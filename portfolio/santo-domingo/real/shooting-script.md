# Santo Domingo — "through my lens" (real footage)

Vertical 9:16, 60–75 s, filmed and narrated by you. This is the piece for form question 3.1:
your camera, your voice, your city. Same fact spine as the animated film, so the two work as a pair
("the city I walk" / "the city, drawn").

**Every fact below is checked** (UNESCO World Heritage Centre, site 526; UNESCO Intangible Heritage
lists for merengue 2016 and bachata 2019). Lines in `[brackets]` are yours to fill with something
true from your own life — that is what makes it *your* lens.

## Gear and settings

- Phone is enough. 4K or 1080p, 30 fps, **vertical**. Lock exposure on the brightest wall (tap and hold).
- Film at golden hour: 6:30–8:00 a.m. (empty streets, warm coral-stone walls) or the last hour before sunset.
- Hold each shot **5 seconds minimum**, move slowly, one movement per shot (walk in, tilt up, or pan — not all three).
- Record 10 s of **room tone / street sound** at each location — it glues the edit together.
- Museums (Alcázar de Colón, Fortaleza): check the photo/video rules at the ticket desk before filming inside.
- If a person is identifiable and speaks to camera, ask first and get a "yes" on camera. Crowds in public are fine.

## Edit (CapCut, free, is enough)

1. Lay the VO first, then cut pictures to it. Cut on footsteps or on a beat.
2. Keep your street sound under the VO at about −18 dB; bring the music clip up at 46 s.
3. Burned-in subtitles (CapCut → Auto captions), placed in the middle third of the screen, not the bottom
   (the Reels/TikTok UI covers the bottom quarter).
4. First 2 seconds must hook: feet stepping + your first line. No logo intro.
5. Last frame: "Filmed by `[Your Name]` · Santo Domingo, `[month]` 2026". Hold 2 s.
6. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps.
