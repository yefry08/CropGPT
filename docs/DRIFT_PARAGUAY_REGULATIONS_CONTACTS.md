## 1. REGULACIONES DE DRONES EN PARAGUAY

### Autoridad Principal: DINAC (Dirección Nacional de Aeronáutica Civil)

**Misión**: Regular aviación civil, incluyendo drones (UAS = Unmanned Aircraft Systems)

**Contacto**:
- **Sitio Web**: www.dinac.gov.py
- **Email**: [Check website]
- **Dirección**: Asunción, Paraguay
- **Teléfono**: [Check current]

**Regulación Clave**: Resolución DINAC sobre operaciones de RPA (Remotely Piloted Aircraft)

### Current Status (as of 2026)
- ✅ Drones pequeños (<25kg) permitidos en uso educativo/hobbyista
- ✅ Operador debe ser mayor de 16 años
- ⚠️ Flying zones: Restringido en zonas urbanas, aeropuertos
- ⚠️ Flying altitude: Max 120m AGL (Above Ground Level)
- ⚠️ Visual Line of Sight (VLOS): Operador debe ver drone en todo momento

### Para DRIFT Drones (Educational Program)

**Compliance checklist:**
- [ ] Register program with DINAC (request educational exemption)
- [ ] Ensure all instructors obtain **Drone Pilot Remote License** (if required)
- [ ] Flying zones: Use school grounds / private property
- [ ] Altitude: Keep flights under 120m
- [ ] Insurance: Liability coverage for educational operations
- [ ] Documentation: Keep records of flights, incidents, maintenance

**Educational Exemption**: Contact DINAC directly to request educational use exemption. Most countries grant these for school programs with proper safety protocols.

**Key Documents to Prepare**:
1. Safety manual (Spanish)
2. Curriculum overview
3. Insurance certificate
4. Liability forms (student + parent signatures)
5. Incident reporting procedures

   - Contact: Municipal sports departments

   - Private space, no permits needed

### Safety Requirements
- Flat, clear area (minimum 50m x 50m for tournaments)
- No obstacles (trees, power lines)
- Fenced if public access possible
- Good lighting (evening tournaments)
- Weather: No strong wind (>15 mph), no rain

## 13. COMMON PITFALLS & HOW TO AVOID

### Regulatory Mistakes
❌ Starting programs without DINAC approval  
✅ Call DINAC in Week 1, get written exemption before flying

❌ Flying near airports/cities without permits  
✅ Use private school grounds only

❌ No insurance  
✅ Obtain liability insurance before first class

### School Partnership Mistakes
❌ Contacting only budget directors (price-focused)  
✅ Contact innovation/academic directors (mission-focused)

❌ No clear ROI story  
✅ Prepare: student outcomes, marketing value, curriculum alignment

❌ Overselling technical complexity  
✅ Emphasize: fun, engagement, modern skills

### Operational Mistakes
❌ Not having backup drones  
✅ Budget 20% extra drones for breakage/repairs

❌ Poor safety protocols  
✅ Write comprehensive safety manual (Spanish), enforce strictly

❌ No clear communication with schools  
✅ Weekly updates, progress reports, transparency
