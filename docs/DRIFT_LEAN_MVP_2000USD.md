## 💡 Cost-Cutting Hacks

### Hardware Savings
- **Buy used drones**: Facebook Marketplace, OLX Paraguay
  - DJI Tello used: $30-50 (save $250 on drones)
- **Refurbished laptops**: Cheaper than new
  - Can find good ones for $200-300 in Paraguay
- **Borrow space**: School gym, sports fields (free)
- **Community resources**: Library wifi, parent connections

### Time/Labor Savings
- **You teach initially**: Don't hire instructor yet
- **Crowdsource curriculum**: YouTube tutorials (free), reference publicly available drone programs
- **Student volunteers**: Year 2+, have senior students help teach juniors (free labor)
- **Parent volunteers**: One tech-savvy parent co-teaches coding (free)

### Marketing Savings
- **DIY everything**: Social media posts, flyers, email
- **User-generated content**: Students + parents share photos
- **Free PR**: Local newspapers love education stories (send press release)
- **Word-of-mouth**: Best marketing, costs $0

### Software Savings
- **All free**: Python, Blockly, DJI Tello SDK, simulators
- **Free hosting**: Wix starter plan ($0 first month)
- **Free communication**: Gmail, WhatsApp for school contact

## 🎓 Solo Teaching Tips

### Managing 20 Students Alone
- Split into 2 groups (10 each, different activities)
- Group 1: Flying drones while Group 2: Coding on laptops
- Rotate every 30 minutes
- Recruit 1-2 parent volunteers to help supervise

### Keeping Costs Low
- Reuse curriculum every cohort (built once, used 5x)
- Students help troubleshoot (peer learning)
- Use simulators for intro (saves drone battery)
- Document everything (videos become future teaching material)

### Your Advantage
- You're founder = authentic passion + story
- Parents see you're invested (teaching yourself)
- Students connect with founder energy
- Better word-of-mouth than hiring unknown instructor

## 💬 Common Questions (Lean Model)

**Q: Is $2,000 really enough?**  
A: Yes. You're MVP-focused. Proven many startups with less. Once you validate model (Month 3), revenue reinvests and scales.

**Q: Won't students notice I'm teaching the first time?**  
A: They'll love it. Authentic founder energy beats polished instructor. Plus, you're learning WITH them.

**Q: What if I can't get a school in Month 2?**  
A: Extend timeline. Use Month 2-3 to validate. If no school interest after 10 calls, pivot or rethink approach.

**Q: Should I hire someone immediately?**  
A: No. Prove model first. Hiring before revenue is risky. Month 5+ when revenue is stable, then hire help.

**Q: What about Drone Soccer equipment?**  
A: DIY first. Mark goal posts with cones. Keep it simple. Professional field can wait until Year 2.

**Q: When do I get paid salary?**  
A: Month 4-5 once revenue covers ops costs. You're investing your time first 3 months.
