## 🛠️ What You'll Need

### Personal
- Time commitment: 50% initially, 100% by month 3
- Passion for STEM education + drones
- Basic project management skills
- Spanish fluency (essential)
- Paraguay connections (helpful but not required)

### Technical
- Drones: DJI Tello (~$100 each, need 15-20)
- Computers: For coding curriculum (refurbished OK)
- Software: Free (Python, Blockly, DJI SDK)
- Flying space: School grounds (50m x 50m clear area)

### Business
- Business registration in Paraguay
- Liability insurance
- Tax ID (RUC)
- Bank account
- Website (basic)

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls (Don't Do These)

❌ Skip DINAC contact (you'll fly illegally)  
❌ Oversell complexity to schools (keep it simple)  
❌ Hire too many people too fast (lean at first)  
❌ Ignore safety (one accident = end of business)  
❌ Launch without school validation (test first)  
❌ Price too low (undermine premium positioning)  
❌ Forget insurance (catastrophic risk)
