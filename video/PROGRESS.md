## Decisions

### Assets and data
- `assets/` is Remotion's public folder, so captures, narration and music live in the paths the brief asks for.
- `assets/manifest.json` is generated by `scripts/prepare.ts` before each render. It is committed so that `studio` and `test-render` work without preparing.
- `timeline.ts` is shared by the video and the `.srt` export, so the burned subtitles and the files have the same timing.

### Subtitles
- Without narration, each scene's time is split among its cues in proportion to text length.
- In scene 5 the figure cues go only into the `.srt` files and are not burned in, because the same figure is already on screen in large type. The OAS line is burned in.

### Content restrictions
- No people, no logos. "Contractor AI" appears as text with the brand gradient. The OAS is mentioned as text only, without its logo.
- The scene 2 bars are a schematic with no values, labeled "Illustration". They are not a capture or real data.
- On-screen text uses only phrases from the script (or their translation): no partnerships, clients, team or users.
- Severity colors (red/amber/green) appear in the scene 2 bar, the "Warrants review" badge and the scene 4 legend.

### Closing scene
- Until there is a real URL, the closing scene shows `[URL PENDIENTE]`.
